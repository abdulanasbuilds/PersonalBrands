The two days

What happens over the two days?

We take your company apart, function by function.
Not literally.
That would upset accounting.
But we are going to look at the six places where AI can fundamentally change how the business operates.

Operations
Remove recurring work from the system.
Find the processes eating hours every week and identify what can be compressed, automated, delegated to AI, or removed altogether.
Sales
Put AI inside the revenue engine.
Improve research, preparation, follow-up, speed, and sales output without creating robotic customer experiences.
Talent
Know what to hire, automate, and amplify.
Before adding another person, understand whether the work needs a new hire, a better process, or AI.
Finance
Find where AI actually moves margin.
Better analysis.
Faster information.
Lower operating cost.
More visibility into where money is leaking and where leverage lives.
Marketing
Produce more without becoming the content department.
Research, ideation, production, distribution.
Build systems that let a smaller team create more without lowering the bar.
Leadership
Build a company that needs fewer decisions from you.
Improve how information moves through the business so your team can make better decisions without constantly waiting for the founder.
