The five shifts

The sequence, in order.

01

From Operator to Architect

I spend 97% of my time building Martell Ventures, and almost none of it doing the work. You climb from doing → reviewing → strategy → architect. The higher you climb, the more you’re worth.

02

From Bottleneck to Builder of Leaders

The “only-I-can-do-it” belief caps a company at about $300K the first time. Then it shows up again at every level you reach. You break it for good by building leaders who run the business without you. The test: you could leave for six months and they’d 10x it.

03

From Cash-Flow Machine to Sellable Asset

Built a $0 → $100M portfolio. A cash-flow machine pays you. A sellable asset pays you, runs without you, and adds to your net worth at a 3–5x multiple.

04

From Tinkerer to Tech-Enabled Leader

This is where you stop saying “I use AI” and start saying “it runs”, a department, a workflow, a process. Build the machine that runs the machine.

05

From Culture by Default to Culture by Design

You decide how you do business: who’s in the room, the standard, what you won’t tolerate. A culture designed on purpose, with leaders who raise the bar without you pushing.
